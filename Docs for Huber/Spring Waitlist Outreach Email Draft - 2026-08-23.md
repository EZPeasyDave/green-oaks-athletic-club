# Spring Waitlist Outreach Email Draft - 2026-08-23

*For Coach Izenstark to send to parents whose kids were waitlisted for the
spring 2026 session and are now in grades 3–5. This is a warm list — they
already asked once and were turned away.*

---

## Before sending

> **BCC.** Every parent address goes in BCC. These families don't know each
> other, and they shouldn't see each other's email addresses.

**Link to `greenoaksathleticclub.com`, not the Stripe URL.** The payment link
deactivates the instant it hits 50, and a deactivated link shows parents a dead
end. The site keeps working either way and has the Register button right on it —
that's one extra click for a much safer failure mode. This is the same rule the
social posts follow.

**One decision:** the draft says they're hearing first. Only keep that line if
it's true — if the flyer and social posts are already out, cut it. An untrue
head start is the kind of thing a parent notices and remembers.

---

```
Grades 3–5: your spot is finally here
```

```
greenoaksathleticclub.com

Any questions, just reply to this email.

Hope to see them in October,

Coach Izenstark
Green Oaks Athletic Club
greenoaksathleticclub.com
```

---

## Why it reads the way it does

- **Leads with the apology-shaped hook.** "We ran out of room and I didn't
  forget" is the single most persuasive thing you can say to someone who was
  turned away. It's also true.
- **No fake scarcity.** "Capped at 50, first come, I can't hold a spot" is the
  actual mechanic — the Stripe link deactivates at 50. Saying it plainly is
  both honest and more motivating than invented urgency.
- **Nothing about the November 13 early release, pickup, or what to bring.**
  That's what the welcome email is for, after they've paid. This email has one
  job: get them to click.
